Pane identity palette

--bp-pane-1 … --bp-pane-10

Ten hues on one OKLCH lightness and one chroma — 0.760/0.105 on dark, 0.550/0.115 on light. Separation is carried entirely by hue, so no pane shouts louder than another and none of them competes with page content. Fixed lightness is also what makes them survive rasterising: an agent reading a composite PNG discriminates hue reliably at equal value, and each colour clears 3:1 against both chrome grounds.
